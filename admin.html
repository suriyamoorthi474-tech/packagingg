How to update the website

1. Change company details and boxes below.   2. Press Download data.json.   3. Upload that file to your hosting, replacing the old data.json next to index.html. The website updates straight away. Your edits are also kept in this browser until you discard them.

Company details

Shown in the header, footer and contact section. WhatsApp number: country code first, digits only (for India, 91 then the number).

Boxes

Sizes are in cm. Photos show on the site beside the live size drawing.
